Welcome to Frame of Mind, a site for people who like to think a little harder about the films they watch, the books they read, and the lives they are living. This is not a place for jargon or gatekeeping. It is a place for clear, honest writing about the ideas that actually change how you see things.

Everything here is organized around five simple lenses. In Film & TV, we look past the plot to what a story is really doing. In Philosophy, we take the big questions out of the lecture hall and into everyday life. In Psychology, we explore why people think, feel, and act the way they do. In Books & Ideas, we sit with the works and thinkers worth knowing. And in Life & Meaning, we bring it all back to the question of how to live well.

Whether you came for a film breakdown or a few minutes of good thinking with your coffee, we are glad you are here. Browse the latest posts below, and see where the ideas take you.
